# In Short

César Soto Valero is a Lead Data Scientist at [SEB](https://sebgroup.com), one of the largest banks in the Nordics, where he drives AI solutions within the Investment Banking division.

He works on applied AI for finance: financial document intelligence, agentic workflows, and the evaluation of AI systems in a regulated environment.

Before that, he spent three years in Financial Crime Prevention at SEB, building and maintaining real-time AI/ML systems used by millions of customers.

He holds a PhD in Computer Science from KTH Royal Institute of Technology (2023), has authored [30+ research papers](/publications) with 1,000+ citations, and is a [keynote speaker](/talks) and [open-source author](/software) based in Stockholm, Sweden.

# What I Work On

I build AI systems for a bank, where a wrong number is worse than no number.

That shapes everything I care about:

- **Financial document intelligence.** Getting reliable data out of quarterly reports, credit memos, broker research, and spreadsheets with multimodal LLMs. I explain when to retrieve and when to extract in [Reading Financial Documents with AI](/blog/reading-financial-documents-with-ai.html).
- **Agentic workflows and multi-agent systems.** Choosing the simplest design that works: a validated workflow first, autonomous agents only when they earn it. See my notes on [multi-agent patterns for investment finance](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/cesarsotovalero_here-are-4-multi-agent-ai-patterns-for-investment-activity-7514180605731954688-hBLF) and on [agentic workflows vs. agents](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/cesarsotovalero_in-finance-many-valuable-use-cases-can-be-activity-7508122387452944384-jeIv).
- **Knowledge graphs and GraphRAG.** Connecting facts spread across several reports, such as subsidiaries, guarantees, and counterparties. See [GraphRAG for financial documents](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/cesarsotovalero_the-most-valuable-ai-driven-insights-in-finance-activity-7513963765097279489-kxPU).
- **Evals and grounding.** Measuring whether an AI answer is supported by the source, not whether it reads well. See [why grounding is the main risk of financial agents](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/cesarsotovalero_financial-agents-often-produce-nice-looking-activity-7524411166778036224-H2SZ).
- **Real-time fraud detection.** Machine learning for transaction monitoring under strict latency and class-imbalance constraints. See my [survey of techniques](/blog/from-classical-ml-to-dnns-and-gnns-for-real-time-financial-fraud-detection.html) and my guide to [evaluation metrics](/blog/evaluation-metrics-for-real-time-financial-fraud-detection-ml-models.html).
- **Trustworthy AI and governance.** Making AI actions provable, authorized, and auditable, including the security of the software supply chain that agents depend on.
