## TL;DR

I'm a Lead Data Scientist at [SEB](https://sebgroup.com), where I drive AI solutions within the Investment Banking division.

My focus is [applied AI for finance](/ai-for-finance): reading financial documents with LLMs, building agentic workflows, and evaluating AI systems so a bank can trust them.

Before that, I spent three years in Financial Crime Prevention at SEB, building real-time AI/ML systems used by millions of customers.

I'm also a [computer scientist](/publications) with a PhD from KTH and a hands-on [software engineer](/software).

I'm on a mission to help engineers learn, build, and grow faster (now with AI).

Check out my [personal blog](/blog), [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCR4rI98w6-MqYoCS6jR9LGg), or connect with me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/cesarsotovalero).

Here's my [one-page résumé](/files/CV/cesar-resume.pdf).

More importantly, these are [my professional values](/my-professional-values).

## Experience

- **September 2026 to present:** Lead Data Scientist, Investment Banking division, SEB. I drive AI solutions within the Investment Banking division.
- **April 2023 to August 2026:** Data Engineer, then Data Scientist and Senior Data Scientist, Financial Crime Prevention, SEB. I built and maintained real-time AI/ML systems for fraud detection, used by millions of customers.
- **2018 to 2023:** PhD researcher in Computer Science, KTH Royal Institute of Technology. Research on software debloating and the software supply chain, funded by WASP.
